Assumptions, Legal Warnings & Tax Flags
Critical compliance notes for ITR-2 / ITR-3 submission:
Budget 2024 Transition Cut-off
12.5% LTCG, 20% STCG, and ₹1.25L exemption statutory rates apply strictly to transfers executed on or after 23-Jul-2024. Pre-July transfers for FY2022-23 and FY2023-24 are computed at standard rates (10% LTCG, 15% STCG, ₹1L exemption).
STCG Loss Set-off (Sec 70/74)
FY2024-25 STCG loss of -₹46,41,568.20 is eligible to be set off against taxable LTCG (₹1,31,60,608.96) under Section 70, reducing tax liability, or carried forward for 8 consecutive Assessment Years under Section 74.
Foreign Equity Classification Scope
Schedule CG strictly covers Indian listed equities. Foreign US equities (Interactive Brokers) are unlisted for Indian tax purposes (24-month holding period threshold for LTCG), and capital gains are computed separately under applicable slab rates.
Form 67 & Schedule TR Checklist
To legally claim DTAA relief (₹2,06,847.15) on US dividends, Form 67 must be e-filed on the IT portal prior to filing the return, accompanied by 1042-S TDS certificates and verified in Schedule TR and Schedule FSI.
